Privacy Policy

OpenClaw Google Integration is a private, personal application used only by its owner to access the owner's own Google account data.

Google data accessed

Depending on the permissions granted by the owner, the integration may access data from Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Docs, Google Sheets, Google Contacts, and Google Tasks.

How data is used

Google user data is used only to perform actions requested by the owner, such as reading, searching, summarizing, organizing, or presenting the owner's information.

Storage and retention

Data may be processed on systems controlled by the owner and may be retained only as needed for operation, local application state, logs, backups, or troubleshooting.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, rented, used for advertising, or shared with third parties for their own purposes.

Security

Access credentials are intended to be kept private and protected. Access to Google data is limited to the permissions granted by the owner.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke the application's access at any time through the Google Account security and permissions settings.

Changes

This policy may be updated if the integration's functionality or data handling changes.
