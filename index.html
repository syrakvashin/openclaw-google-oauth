OpenClaw Google Integration

This is a private, personal integration used by its owner to access the owner's own Google account data through OpenClaw.

Purpose

The integration may access Google services such as Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Contacts, and Tasks in order to read and organize information requested by the account owner.

Access

The integration is not offered as a public service. It is intended only for the owner's personal use.

Privacy

See the Privacy Policy for details about how Google user data is handled.
